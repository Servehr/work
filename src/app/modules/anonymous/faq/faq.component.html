Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email address to receive a reset link.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your plan from your account settings at any time without extra fees.
